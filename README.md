# Patricia Yuen Dermatology — Acne Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

AcneDoctor.sg is an acne and acne-scar landing page for Patricia Yuen Dermatology at Paragon Medical Centre, Singapore. It introduces Dr Patricia Yuen’s specialist care and guides prospective patients towards appointment enquiries.

The redesigned homepage improves readability, brings clinic details forward and explains the care process without requiring visitors to expand sections.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. More Readable Text and Buttons

Darker text, larger button labels and a minimum text size of 15px improve content readability. Previously, some action labels were 12px, while light-grey paragraphs lacked contrast against ivory backgrounds.

### 2. Earlier Access to Clinic Details

Location information now appears near the hero, alongside a direct link to full opening hours. Persistent Call and WhatsApp buttons provide mobile contact options.

Previously, visitors had to reach the bottom of the page to find essential clinic details. The revised placement makes visit planning and appointment enquiries easier to initiate.

### 3. Visible Treatment Guidance

Six clear care pathways and three openly displayed treatment stages cover breakouts, maintenance, and residual marks or scars. These address explanations previously hidden in collapsed sections, helping visitors understand the care process without additional interaction.

## Design Improvements

- **Typography and contrast:** Darker text and larger labels improve readability, with no text below 15px.
- **Layout and hierarchy:** Clinic location information appears near the introduction.
- **Content presentation:** Care pathways and visible stages provide a clear structure for treatment guidance.
- **Mobile experience:** Persistent Call and WhatsApp buttons keep contact options accessible.
