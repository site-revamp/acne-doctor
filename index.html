The treatment approach

A plan shaped around
your skin.

The clinic’s approach considers active acne, ongoing control and residual marks or scars. The sequence and treatments depend on your assessment.

01

Manage the breakouts.

Address active acne with prescribed treatment. Dr Yuen may consider additional procedures according to your skin’s needs.

Options discussed may include Prescription creams, oral medication and selected light-based treatments.
02

Maintain progress.

Build a dermatologist-guided skincare routine and review how your skin responds over time.

Care may involve Maintenance prescriptions, skincare guidance and selected in-clinic treatments.
03

Address what remains.

Discuss scars, pigmentation or redness after acne, with treatment selected for the concern being assessed.

Options discussed may include Chemical peels, laser or light-based procedures and other appropriate treatments.

A consultation is needed to decide which treatments are suitable. Not every patient will need every stage or procedure.

Speak with the clinic
Can adults seek help for acne?

Yes. The clinic sees teenagers and adults with acne concerns, including recurring breakouts and marks or scars left behind. Your consultation helps determine the appropriate care.

Are breakouts and acne scars treated the same way?

The clinic’s approach distinguishes active acne management, maintenance and treatment of residual marks or scars. Dr Yuen will assess your concerns and discuss the appropriate sequence and options.

How soon will I see a change?

Response varies with the concern, severity and treatment. Discuss expected timelines and review appointments with Dr Yuen during your consultation.

Will treatment involve downtime?

Downtime and aftercare depend on the procedure. Ask about recovery, suitability and aftercare before deciding on a treatment.
